Reinforcement Learning · Computer Vision

Project ML Racing

A Unity racing environment where a PPO reinforcement learning agent learns to drive using computer vision and vehicle telemetry.

UnityPythonPyTorchOpenCVNumPy
Watch on YouTube →

A personal project where I built a Unity-based racing environment and connected it to a PPO reinforcement learning agent that learns to drive using computer vision and vehicle telemetry. What began as a simple experiment became one of the most engaging learning experiences I've had.

The agent can currently make its way around the track — though, for now, my keyboard driving still beats it. There's plenty left to do before it can truly race competitively, and that's exactly what makes it fun.

What I explored

  • Computer vision with Canny edge detection for lane detection
  • CNN-based lane detection using a MobileNet model
  • Data collection & classification to train the model
  • Unity-to-Python integration for camera & telemetry
  • PPO reinforcement learning & reward shaping
  • Model fine-tuning and debugging the full pipeline
The hardest moments came when the model behaved unexpectedly and I had to properly understand the integration between the CNN, the PPO agent, the observations, and the reward structure.

Multiplayer game · Red Cross Youth

Multiplayer Disaster Response Simulation

A four-player Unity game for a drone workshop. Each role sees only part of the picture, so the team has to talk to find survivors and deliver supplies in time.

UnityC#Netcode for GameObjectsUnity Transport

Click a photo to enlarge it and read what’s happening.

A four-player disaster-response game I built in Unity for a Red Cross Youth drone workshop. Each player takes on a different role and only sees the information relevant to that role, so the team has to talk and coordinate to find survivors and deliver supplies before time runs out.

  • Drone OperatorFlies the drone to scan for hidden survivors and supply crates.
  • CommanderReceives discoveries on a live tactical map and directs the team.
  • Drivers (×2)Navigate the flooded area, collect objectives and return them to HQ.
  • Shared missionTimer, scoring and crash penalties that the whole team works against.

What I built

  • LAN multiplayer with Netcode for GameObjects (direct-IP host/client)
  • Server-authoritative mission state with client-authoritative vehicle movement
  • Role selection, ready-up and countdown synced across clients
  • Role-specific cameras, UI and controls
  • Drone flight, camera control and object scanning
  • Real-time tactical map tracking players and objectives
  • Vehicle driving, collection and delivery mechanics
  • Keyboard and gamepad support
The biggest challenge wasn't Unity itself but balancing the user experience with the lesson objectives: the game had to be intuitive and fun while still making players rely on one another.

A virtual toy-train city with a Japanese theme, inspired by riding the bullet train and by toy train sets. Lay track on the board, build stations and signals, bridge rivers, tunnel through hills, and watch a town grow around your stations.

It runs in the browser with nothing to install, and it opens with a starter city that is already running, so you can simply watch the trains or climb aboard one.

What's in it

  • Eleven bullet trains running on a 56 × 56 board with four build levels
  • Express and local services that overtake and couple at stations
  • Stations with platform doors, queueing passengers, staff and departure melodies
  • Rivers with bridges, hills with tunnels, roads and level crossings
  • Highways on pillars with ramps, flyovers and traffic
  • Passengers whose journeys grow the town around each station
  • A ride-along camera with driver, chase and window views
  • Four seasons, day and night, and sound generated in the browser

Hex Farm

A 3D hex-tile farming and settlement game in the browser. Grow a camp into a town while rabbits raid your crops, foxes hunt and neighbouring villages trade with you or raid you.

ReactThree.jsViteGitHub Pages
Play it →Code →

Click a screenshot to enlarge it.

A 3D hex-tile farming and settlement game that runs entirely in the browser. Start with a camp on a hex map and grow it into a town: farm, cut wood, quarry stone, catch horses and claim land, while rabbits raid your crops, foxes hunt, and neighbouring villages trade with you or raid you.

The world keeps running while you're away and catches up when you return. Your settlement saves to a small file you can load anywhere, with no account or server needed.

What's in it

  • An hourly clock, four seasons, a sky driven by real sun position, dusk lamps and moonlight
  • Workers who walk to each job, queue up, mend what breaks and forage when idle
  • Rabbits, foxes, horses, goats and hens, with a predator–prey balance tuned over three simulated years
  • Territory that grows as you build, plus raiders and peaceful settlers
  • Progression from Camp to Homestead, Hamlet, Village and Town, with tools, skills, healing and defences

AdminHub

A web app for a team’s everyday admin work: room bookings, duty rosters, attendance and tasks, all behind one sign-in. Runs on the Python standard library and SQLite.

PythonSQLiteHTMLCSSJavaScript

Click a screenshot to enlarge it. All names and records shown are made-up sample data.

A web app for a team's everyday admin work: room bookings, duty rosters, shift planning, attendance, fitness tests, vehicles and tasks, all behind one sign-in with a home dashboard that shows what needs your attention today.

It runs on nothing but the Python standard library and SQLite, so it needs nothing extra installed: one command starts it in the browser, with sample data ready to explore.

What it does

  • Meeting rooms on one calendar, bookings that can't clash, check-in and check-out
  • Duty roster with auto-generated drafts, availability checks and duty swaps
  • Shift planning with desk-duty rules and minimum manpower per shift
  • Attendance headcount, plus leave and course approvals
  • Fitness test results, forecasts and session booking
  • Kanban tasks with team workload and deadline alerts
  • Room equipment, fault reports and a repair workflow
  • Role-based permissions and a timestamped activity log
